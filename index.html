About

I am a doctoral student at the Guanghua School of Management, Peking University, where I began my Ph.D. in 2024. My training in management science shapes the questions I ask about AI: what evidence was available, which constraints mattered, and what would have changed the decision?

I also study the people behind AI systems. Two abstracts accepted for oral presentation at INDL-9 explore how workers can verify data provenance and participate in the governance of AI supply chains.
